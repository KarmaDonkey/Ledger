# Committee register

> Meeting rhythm and deadlines are real; **mandates, members and thresholds are FICTIONAL** for the proof of concept.

## Order and rhythm
The path is always in this order (not every item goes all the way):
```
TC → GAAC → GEC → RCC → Board
```

| Committee | Meets | Pre-read deadline |
|---|---|---|
| **TC** – Treasury Committee | Every **Thursday** | **Monday** the same week (3 days before) |
| **GAAC** – Group Asset Allocation Committee | Every **Tuesday** | **Thursday** the week before (5 days before) |
| **GEC** – Group Executive Committee | Every **Monday** | **Friday** the week before (3 days before) |
| **RCC** – Board Risk and Compliance Committee | **Wednesday in the 3rd week** of each month | **9 days** before |
| **Board** | **3rd Thursday** of each month | **9 days** before |

Rules of thumb:
- "9 days" = calendar days. If the deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the previous business day.
- A meeting that falls on a public holiday is assumed cancelled unless the calendar says otherwise.
- **The committee calendar always wins** over these rules: meetings get moved, extra meetings are added, and **ad hoc committees** (e.g. a steering group or a model committee) can appear. Treat an ad hoc committee as an extra step in the path where the user places it; ask for its deadline if it is not in the calendar.
- **RCC and Board often fall in the same week** (Wednesday/Thursday). The Board's deadline then comes *before* RCC meets, so an item that needs RCC first usually goes to RCC the month before the Board. Point this out when planning.

## Common format (all committees)
- **One template** (see `mallar.md`): **Executive summary · Resolution · The matter at hand (max 5 pages)** · optional appendix.
- **Language**: English by default. Swedish only if the user says so.
- **Booking**: the item must be booked on the agenda with the secretary; status is tracked in the calendar (column `Booked`).

## TC – Treasury Committee
- **Chair**: Group Treasurer
- **Members (roles)**: Head of ALM, Head of Funding, Head of Market Risk (2nd line), Group Financial Controller
- **Mandate (summary)**: Liquidity and funding, interest rate risk in the banking book, FX hedging, investment portfolio. Decides within limits set by GAAC; prepares limit changes and funding strategy for GAAC.
- **Audience profile**: Expert – technical depth is fine, but the paper must still lead with the resolution.
- **Usually asks about**: assumptions, scenarios, limit utilisation, market conditions, hedge effectiveness.

## GAAC – Group Asset Allocation Committee
- **Chair**: CFO
- **Members (roles)**: Group Treasurer, CRO, Head of Group Finance, Head of Capital Management, Head of Tax
- **Mandate (summary)**: Allocation of capital, balance sheet and liquidity across business areas and asset classes; investment and asset allocation strategy; ALM framework; capital and funding planning; limits within Board-approved risk appetite. Prepares items for GEC and RCC.
- **Audience profile**: Expert/Management
- **Usually asks about**: return on allocated capital, capital and P&L impact, consistency with financial plan and risk appetite, rating impact, opportunity cost versus other uses of capital.

## GEC – Group Executive Committee
- **Chair**: CEO
- **Members (roles)**: CFO, CRO, COO, CIO, Chief Compliance Officer, Head of HR, business area heads
- **Mandate (summary)**: CEO's decision forum. Strategy execution, investments above GAAC's mandate, organisational changes, items that go to the Board.
- **Audience profile**: Management
- **Usually asks about**: business and customer impact, cost and benefit, priority against other initiatives, execution risk, reputation, who owns it.

## RCC – Board Risk and Compliance Committee
- **Chair**: Non-executive Board member
- **Members (roles)**: 3–4 Board members; CRO and Chief Compliance Officer attend; Head of Internal Audit attends
- **Mandate (summary)**: Prepares risk and compliance matters for the Board: risk appetite, risk policies, capital and liquidity adequacy (ICAAP/ILAAP), material risk exposures.
- **Audience profile**: Board – risk-literate but not specialists. No unexplained jargon.
- **Usually asks about**: risk appetite fit, downside scenarios, regulatory view, control environment, lessons from past incidents.

## Board
- **Chair**: Chair of the Board
- **Mandate (summary)**: Strategy, risk appetite, policies, major investments and transactions, approval of ICAAP/ILAAP.
- **Audience profile**: Board – "SvD Näringsliv / FT Lex reader": an intelligent generalist.
- **Usually asks about**: strategic fit, why now, what could go wrong, customers and reputation, regulator, how this will be followed up.
- **Note**: Items normally have passed GEC and, for risk matters, RCC. The paper should state which bodies have reviewed it and their view.

# Writing rules – English

House style (fictional): **British English**, plain-language principles (Plain English Campaign, EU *Clear Writing* guide).

## Structure
- Bottom line up front: recommendation in the first paragraph.
- Informative headings ("We recommend raising the limit to SEK 200m" rather than "Recommendation").
- One idea per paragraph; bullets for parallel items, not for arguments.

## Sentences and words
- Average sentence length **15–20 words**.
- Active voice: "We recommend" not "It is recommended".
- Prefer verbs to nouns: "decide" not "make a decision", "analyse" not "carry out an analysis".
- Plain words: "use" (not "utilise"), "start" (not "commence"), "about" (not "approximately" in running text), "because" (not "due to the fact that").
- Avoid Swenglish: "eventually" ≠ "eventuellt" (use "possibly"), "actual" ≠ "aktuell" (use "current"), "control" ≠ "kontrollera" when you mean "check", "competence" → "skills/expertise".

## Numbers and dates
- Currency: **SEK 12m**, **SEK 1.2bn**, **SEK 450k** (decimal point, not comma).
- Thousands: **40,000**.
- Percentages: **3%** (no space) in British business style.
- Dates: **12 March 2027**. Avoid 03/12/2027 (ambiguous).

## Spelling (British)
- -ise (organise, prioritise), -our (colour, behaviour), -re (centre), "programme" (but computer "program"), "licence" (noun) / "license" (verb).

## Abbreviations
- Spell out on first use: "Board Risk and Compliance Committee (RCC)". Always explain committee acronyms on first use in papers going to the Board.

## Tone
- Direct, factual, no hype. Hedge only where there is real uncertainty – and quantify it.
